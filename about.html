About Me

Friendly, playful, and creative—with purpose.

Tatiana DeGuise

Graphic & Web Designer

I design experiences that feel approachable and work clearly.

I'm a Graphic Design & Media Arts student at Southern New Hampshire University with a concentration in web design. Throughout the program, I've developed experience in responsive web design, UX/UI, branding, social media content, advertising, and print production.

My professional background in operations, customer service, management, and project coordination has also strengthened my organization, communication, attention to detail, and problem-solving skills. I bring those strengths into every design project.

My personal brand is built around three qualities: creative, friendly, and playful. I use purple and gray, rounded shapes, clean layouts, and thoughtful visual hierarchy to create work that feels personal without losing professionalism.
